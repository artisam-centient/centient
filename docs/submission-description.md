# Centient — Train AI, cent by cent

Human preference data is the fuel behind every frontier AI model, and the people who produce it — comparing two AI responses and judging which is better — are paid worst and last. AI labs pay $1–$10 per human judgment, yet the global crowd that generates it is excluded by payment rails that can't economically move five cents across borders on cards or wires. Centient closes that gap. A contributor connects a wallet, compares two AI responses, picks the better one, writes a one-line reason, and earns a micro-reward in USDC (default 0.05); AI labs fund campaigns of these tasks and export clean, quality-controlled datasets. This is a genuinely new, **non-speculative** payment flow for Stellar: every labeler is a net-new activated wallet in an emerging market, every task drives real USDC velocity, and every withdrawal routes volume through anchors as USDC converts to local cash. Stellar is the only network that makes it work end-to-end — sub-cent fees make paying labelers cent by cent viable, USDC is native, and the MoneyGram + Circle + regional-anchor corridor uniquely solves the emerging-market cash-out that the entire model depends on. Quality is engineered in — hidden gold-standard tasks, inter-annotator agreement, anti-fraud and spam guards, and prepaid campaign budgets so payouts never exceed funded work, every one verifiable on-ledger. Our growth and Stellar's are the same motion: more labelers → better data → more funded campaigns → more USDC paid out and cashed out.

**For reviewers:** Both sides of the marketplace are built and running — the labeler app and a full admin/campaign console (fund USDC campaigns, bulk-upload tasks, track progress, export datasets). The Celo→Stellar migration is complete: the platform runs on USDC over Stellar with wallet-based sign-in and an accumulate-then-withdraw ledger, where earnings accrue off-chain and settle on-chain once at cash-out, so on-chain volume tracks real payouts and someone can start labeling before ever funding a wallet. Our Stellar integration roadmap is phased and deliberately reuses existing ecosystem solutions rather than reinventing them: **now** — zero-XLM onboarding via sponsored reserves and fee-bump, plus standards-based auth (SEP-10/SEP-1/SEP-53); **next** — SEP-24 anchor cash-out and the Stellar Disbursement Platform for the payout leg, with SEP-38 quotes; **later** — Soroban for on-chain campaign escrow and reputation, and passkey wallets. We are deliberately **not** running our own anchor or issuing a speculative token — being paid in real USDC is the trust moat. The immediate milestone is a Philippines pilot (instant USDC→peso via Coins.ph and MoneyGram) with design-partner AI labs, expanding across APAC and globally by localizing onto the same cash-out rail.
