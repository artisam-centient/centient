## In short

* **North star:** Daily Quality Contributors, meaning people who meet a daily goal while their quality-check accuracy is at least 70%.
* **Fix the first ten minutes:** a sample task before the wallet, Android sign-in tested, demographics after the third answer, and training checks that explain mistakes instead of pausing newcomers on day one.
* **Build a small, forgiving habit:** a daily goal of 5, 10 or 20 answers, Manila-day streaks with freezes, and an empty task pool that never breaks a streak.
* **Reward accuracy, not volume:** quality-weighted XP with a daily cap, and tiers gated well above chance that unlock better work.
* **Be honest on testnet:** XP is not money or a token. Real incentives (load, prizes, bounties, a fixed mainnet bonus, a tier that carries over) cost about ₱187,000 for a six-week season.
* **Guarantee supply:** an evergreen task pool plus free "data grant" campaigns for five design partners, led by Filipino-language AI teams such as Proto, the FilBench researchers and GradeChum.
* **Schedule:** Season 0 (closed) from 16 to 29 November 2026, Season 1 (public) from 11 January to 21 February 2027.

The decisions the owner needs to make are listed in [the plan's §12](onboarding-gamification-plan.md#12-decisions-for-the-owner).
